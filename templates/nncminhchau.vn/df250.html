DF250

The Stealth V-6 four-stroke outboard. 250 horsepower for tournament bass boats, large flat boats, performance pontoons and ski boats.

Explore the motor

BigBlock V6. 4,028 cc.

A 55° V6 with 24 valves and DOHC, built on Suzuki's BigBlock displacement. It gives a powerful hole shot and strong punch through the mid-range.

Precise fuel. Great economy.

Multi-point sequential electronic injection with an oxygen sensor meters fuel to each cylinder, so you get the power you want and good fuel economy on 89 octane.

Where the power meets the water.

An offset driveshaft and two-stage gear reduction (2.08:1) carry the power down to the propeller. Choose from the 17 to 30 propeller range with your dealer, in a 20 in or 25 in shaft.
